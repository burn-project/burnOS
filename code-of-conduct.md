# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a respectful, safe, and inclusive environment for all contributors, maintainers, users, and community members.

Participation in this project is open to people of all backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. Harassment, abuse, discrimination, intimidation, or hostile conduct will not be tolerated.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

* Communicate respectfully and professionally.
* Assume good intent where reasonable.
* Give and receive constructive feedback.
* Respect differing technical opinions and lived experiences.
* Keep discussions focused on the project and its goals.
* Report security issues responsibly and privately where appropriate.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

* Harassment, threats, abuse, or personal attacks.
* Discriminatory language or conduct.
* Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
* Sexualised language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
* Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith argument.
* Encouraging illegal, harmful, or abusive activity.
* Public disclosure of unresolved security vulnerabilities.

## Security and Responsible Disclosure

This project may involve security-sensitive software. Contributors must not publicly disclose vulnerabilities, bypass methods, exploit details, private keys, credentials, or user-sensitive information without approval from the maintainers.

Security concerns should be reported privately through the project’s documented security reporting process.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behaviour, including:

* Warning the participant.
* Editing or removing comments, issues, commits, or pull requests.
* Temporarily or permanently restricting participation.
* Reporting serious abuse to the relevant platform or authority.

Enforcement decisions are made at the discretion of the maintainers and should prioritise the safety, integrity, and sustainability of the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, chat channels, events, and any public or private communication related to the project.

## Reporting

To report a Code of Conduct issue, contact the maintainers through the project’s nominated contact method.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. The identity of reporters will be handled with care and only shared where necessary to investigate or resolve the matter.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community standards and tailored for security-focused software projects.
